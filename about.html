The story

It started with
two names on a door.

In 1991, Mounir Amine Alam and Sonia Achkar Alam opened a small printing shop in Bikfaya. The early jobs were whatever the neighbourhood needed — cards, forms, invitations — printed carefully and delivered on time.

That habit built a reputation, and the reputation built a company. Small jobs became catalogues, catalogues became packaging, and the shop grew into a full-service press for businesses across Maten and Beirut — with a total commitment to the printing needs of the business community.

As the company grew, so did the family's place in it. Elie and Sarah Alam joined after earning degrees in printing management, bringing digital presses and new finishing lines with them. The values on the door didn't change.
